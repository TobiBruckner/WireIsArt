About Me

The Art of Bringing Wire to Life

I'm Federico Gauna, a visual artist passionate about transforming simple strands of wire into sculptures that convey emotions and tell stories. I began my journey in the art world at the early age of 10, when I discovered that with a little wire and a lot of imagination I could bring any idea that crossed my mind to life.

Over the years, I have perfected my technique, exploring different shapes, textures, and scales. Each of my works is meticulously handcrafted, intertwining every strand with dedication and love for the craft. My work seeks to find beauty in minimalism, proving that even the simplest material can become an extraordinary piece of art.

My sculptures have traveled through diverse spaces and have found homes in private collections around the world. Today I continue creating, experimenting, and sharing my passion for this unique art form that has accompanied me throughout my entire life.

— Federico Gauna
